Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

At romitagl.com, your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our website and tools.

The core principle behind all our web tools is complete privacy: all processing happens directly in your browser, and your data never leaves your device.

Rider Fit workout data

Rider Fit saves your selected rounds, unfinished session, and up to 200 completed sessions in local browser storage. This workout data is not uploaded to a server or shared with a fitness service. It is specific to this browser and device.

Use My progress → Clear journal to delete completed sessions. Clear this site's browser data to remove the journal, round settings, and unfinished session together. If storage is blocked, the workout still works, but its data lasts only while the page remains open.

Exercise illustrations, 3D animations and approved video demos are served with the website. No external video player is loaded. Source and license links open external websites only when selected.
